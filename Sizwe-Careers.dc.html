Careers

Build your own practice with us.

We train our own advisers. If you want to build a book in and around Bloemfontein, we bring the accreditation support, the mentorship and the systems — and the practice you build is yours.

What we put behind you

Accreditation, paid for

RE5, class-of-business and product accreditation, with study time and the exam costs covered.
A mentor from day one

You sit with an experienced adviser in real client meetings before you run your own.
Systems and admin support

Quoting, compliance, record-keeping and back-office support so you spend your time with clients.
A book you own

You build your own client base and earn on it. The practice you grow stays yours.
Who does well here

It is not a sales job.

The advisers who last here are the ones who like the planning work and are comfortable being the person a family phones on a bad day.

A matric certificate

A relevant tertiary qualification helps but is not required. We will guide you through the regulatory exams.

Your own transport

Client meetings happen where the client is, across Bloemfontein and the wider Free State.

A clear credit and criminal record

A regulatory requirement for financial services representatives.

Self-discipline

Your diary is your own. That is the best and the hardest part of the job.
