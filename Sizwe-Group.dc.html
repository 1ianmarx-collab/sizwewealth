The group

Three businesses, one plan for you.

Sizwe Wealth is a progressive and client-focused financial services group, founded in 2023 and based in Bloemfontein. Today we advise families, professionals and owner-managed businesses across the Free State.

What sits under the group

Advice, cover, investments and accounting sit under one roof. That means one adviser who knows your whole position, rather than three who each know a piece of it.

Kapital Bluestar

The Sanlam Blue Star advisory practice: short- and long-term insurance, retirement annuities, tax-free savings and income protection.
Sizwe Wealth advisory

Wealth planning and diversified investment work for individuals and businesses building long-term capital.
Kapital Accounting

Bookkeeping, tax returns, payroll, statutory compliance and business advisory for owner-managed businesses.
The Sanlam partnership

An independent practice, with Sanlam behind it.

Blue Star is Sanlam's model for locally owned advisory branches. We run our own practice in the community we live in, and we answer to the clients in it. Behind us sit Sanlam's product range, systems, compliance framework and adviser accreditation — a group that has been in business since 1918.

Practically, that means local accountability with institutional strength: your adviser is down the road, and your policy is with one of Africa's largest financial services groups.

How we work

Four things we hold ourselves to.

Advice before product

We establish what you need before we mention what we sell. If you already have adequate cover, we will tell you so.

Costs stated plainly

Every fee, premium and commission is written down in your plan, in rands, before you sign anything.

One adviser, long term

You keep the same adviser. We measure ourselves on how many clients are still with us in ten years.

Reviewed every year

A plan written once is a plan out of date. Yours gets revisited annually and whenever your life changes.
